## NM AI Research

Independent researcher on AI governance and the economics of the AI build-out.

**Selected work**

- **Public evidence under Article 50.** What twelve consumer AI products
  publicly evidenced against the EU AI Act's transparency clauses, four days
  after the duty applied, every cell linked to its dated artefact.
- **The Model Is a Dependency.** Whether open weights permit an audit or only a
  record of what was run. On numeric extraction from SEC compensation tables, a
  source-attribution control caught none of 106 cases where a figure was
  attached to the wrong source.
- **What admits a document to FineWeb-Edu.** The published quality threshold is
  applied to a rounded integer, so the real admission boundary is a raw score
  above 2.5. 84 million documents, 94 Common Crawl snapshots.
- **AI Capital-Infrastructure Barbell.** Six supply-side risk factors across
  capital, power, Taiwan, packaging, commoditisation and export controls, each
  with a stated condition that would falsify it.
